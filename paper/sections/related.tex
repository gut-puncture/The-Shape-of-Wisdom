\section{Related work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Reading intermediate layers.}
The logit lens~\citep{nostalgebraist2020interpreting} decodes an intermediate state through the model's output layer, and the tuned lens~\citep{belrose2023eliciting} learns a decoder for each layer. Both show predictions sharpening with depth. Future Lens~\citep{pal2023future} and Patchscopes~\citep{ghandeharioun2024patchscopes} read richer content from a single hidden state. All of these read the state through the output vocabulary. Our centroids are fitted from the states themselves, and reading them does not need the answer words' output directions.

\paragraph{How depth is used.}
Early layers gather and detokenise, middle layers build abstract features, and late layers refine a prediction~\citep{lad2024the,csordas2025do,gupta2025how,cheng2024emergence,skean2025layer}. Correct and incorrect predictions follow similar confidence curves across layers~\citep{kim2025on}. In factual recall, information about the relation and then the subject moves to the last position, where attention heads extract the attribute~\citep{geva2023dissecting}. Feed-forward layers act as key--value memories~\citep{geva2020transformer}, and middle-layer feed-forward modules at the subject's last token are causally decisive~\citep{meng2022locating}. Our onset depth marks, for a whole population of questions, the depth at which each model's answer becomes available at the last position.

\paragraph{Representation geometry.}
Concepts in language models have structured geometry~\citep{park2024the,engels2024not,gurnee2023language,gurnee2026when,marks2023the}, and middle layers share content across languages and modalities~\citep{wendler2024do,wu2024the}. In trained classifiers, classes separate by a roughly constant factor per layer~\citep{he2022a}; language models move partway toward neural collapse, in which states gather at their class means, at their top layer~\citep{wu2024linguistic}. The residual stream also contains stable regions in which small changes leave the prediction unchanged~\citep{janiak2024characterizing}, although, almost surely, no two prompts map to the same state~\citep{nikolaou2025language}. Centroids fit both: questions can gather in the directions that carry the answer while staying distinct in others.

\paragraph{What models know but do not say.}
Internal states often encode more than the output shows~\citep{orgad2024llms,gekhman2025insideout,ruscio2026on}. On knowledge benchmarks such as MMLU and ARC, however, this gap is near zero~\citep{park2025bridging}, and internal states mostly reflect what the model recalls rather than what is true~\citep{cheang2026do}. False few-shot demonstrations can override a correct internal answer at a critical layer~\citep{halawi2023overthinking}, and users' suggestions shift answers~\citep{sharma2023towards}. In multiple choice, attention in a few middle layers selects the answer letter, and a sparse set of heads then raises its probability~\citep{wiegreffe2024answer}. The winning option is chosen by its content before it is bound to its letter~\citep{wong2026when}, and models carry a prior over letters~\citep{zheng2023large}.

\paragraph{Steering.}
Adding a direction to the residual stream changes a model's behaviour~\citep{panickssery2023steering,li2023inferencetime}. The moves here are a special case: the direction is the difference between two measured centroids, applied to one question at one layer, and the control is a random move of the same length.
